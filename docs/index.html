What I care about

  • 01Binaries that start instantly, not after a splash screen.
  • 02Installers that don't demand admin rights.
  • 03Settings that survive an update.
  • 04An uninstall that really removes everything.

What I avoid

  • ×Electron wrappers for things that need 5 MB.
  • ×Telemetry I'd never read.
  • ×Background services that phone home.
  • ×Features nobody asked for, shipped loudly.
